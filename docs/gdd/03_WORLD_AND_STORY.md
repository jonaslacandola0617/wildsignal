## 3.1 The world: the Signal Archipelago

A chain of green islands where people and Signari have always lived side by side. Every Signari carries a **signal**: a sound, felt more than heard, that is its own. Long ago a network of **resonance stations** linked those signals across the islands, and the archipelago hummed with them.

Thirty years ago the network fell silent overnight. People call it **the Hush**. Signari scattered and grew wild and wary, villages lost touch with each other, and the stations became ruins. The **Wayfinders** were founded after the Hush: explorers who travel the islands, study the Signari and keep the peace between them and people.

Now the signal is coming back, in pieces, and nobody knows why.

**Day, night and weather** (recommended): a gentle day–night cycle and light weather (rain, mist, storms in some regions). Some Signari appear only at night or in rain, which gives a reason to return to places you've seen.

## 3.3 Characters

The player stays a **silent Wayfinder** (decision below): you choose a name and look, and you answer people through short dialogue choices that change tone, not the plot. This keeps the classic feeling and lets players put themselves in the story.

| Character | Role | Arc |
|---|---|---|
| **Mara Venn** | Wayfinder guide, mentor | Warm, careful, keeps a secret: she and Sol were Wayfinder partners during the Hush. She left; Sol didn't. |
| **Old Fen** | Compass maker at the Compass Workshop | Builds and upgrades the Resonance Compass. Comic relief with a soft heart; he built Sol's first compass too. |
| **Iri** | Liltwood's caretaker | Protective of the forest's Signari, distrusts Wayfinders at first, becomes an ally. |
| **Juno Hale** | The rival — another new Wayfinder | Competitive and cheerful at first; fights you in each region. Shaken by losing, tempted by the Chorus's promise of control, then turns back and helps in the finale. |
| **Bellkeeper Sol** | Leader of the Chorus — the villain | A former Wayfinder and archivist who lost her partner Signari in the Hush. Sympathetic and wrong. |
| **The Chorus** | Sol's followers | They ring **binding bells** that force a Signari's signal into obedience — the cruel opposite of the player's Tune In, which only works with a worn-down Signari and never forces it. |

## 3.4 The story

**Act 1 — The First Signal** (regions 1–2)
A boat brings the new Wayfinder to Lantern Quay. Mara takes you in; Old Fen gives you the Resonance Compass: *"It won't tell you where to go. It will tell you when something is listening."* You choose your first Signari. A signal pulses from Liltwood, and the forest Signari grow restless. At Echo Hollow you wake the first old station and meet the Chorus for the first time: robed figures ringing a bell over a Signari that can't stop shaking.

**Act 2 — The Chorus** (regions 3–4)
Along the coast the Chorus is binding Signari to power the stations. Juno starts to wonder whether they're right: a world where no Signari is ever lost again. In Ashbell Works you find their base and meet Sol. Her plan: restore the whole network and bind every Signari's signal into one song, so the Hush can never happen again.

**Act 3 — The Truth of the Hush** (regions 5–6)
In Gloamfen Marsh, Mara finally tells the truth. The old network was never a harmony: it was built on bound Signari. The Hush happened when the first Wayfinders — Mara and Sol among them — broke the binding to set them free. Sol's partner was too deep in the core and never came back. Sol has spent thirty years trying to reach it.

In the Deep Station under the Caldera, Sol starts the network. You face her partner Signari, bound into the core. Instead of destroying it, you wear it down and **tune in**, as you have with every friend on your team, and its own signal comes through for the first time in thirty years. Sol hears it and lets go of the bells.

**Ending:** the network rings again, but freely: Signari answer it only if they choose to. Sol stays in the Caldera to look after her partner. Juno becomes a Wayfinder for real. Mara returns to the Workshop with Old Fen. The world is open to finish exploring, and the compass hears a new signal from the north (the post-launch Highlands).

**Why it has weight:** the villain isn't evil, she's grieving; the mentor carries guilt; the rival is tempted by safety; and the finale is won by the game's own core mechanic — listening, not breaking.

## 3.5 How the story moves the game

- **Each region** follows the same shape: arrive → a local problem caused by the returning signal → the Chorus is involved → rival fight → guardian → compass upgrade opens the next region.
- **Guardians** are Signari bound by the Chorus. You beat them, then free them with Tune In; they stay in their region as protectors. After the story, some can be recruited through a special trial (detailed in chapter 4).
- **The main story is 3–5 hours** (chapter 1). Side content adds more.

## 3.6 Side content

- **Mara's field book:** research goals for every species (seen, fought, recruited, habitat notes).
- **Villager requests:** small quests in each village (find a lost Signari, deliver crops, fix a lantern).
- **Rare Signari** with special conditions (night, rain, after a story event).
- **Homestead sites** to discover, one or more per region.
- **Wayfinder trials:** optional fight challenges per region for rewards and money.
- **Station echoes:** collectible memory fragments in the ruins that tell the story of the old network and the Hush in more detail.

## 3.7 Tone guide for writers

- Warm first, sad underneath. Villages are friendly and funny; the sadness lives in the ruins and in what people don't say.
- No one dies on screen. Loss is shown through absence: empty houses, Signari waiting by a door, a broken compass.
- Short lines. Text is read, not voiced. Most lines fit two lines of the dialogue box.
- The Chorus are people, not monsters. They believe they're helping.

## 3.8 What carries over from the pilot

The current playable village becomes **Hearthgrove**, part of region 1. Mara's field book, the cottage rest spot, WS_HOUSE_001 and the existing wildlife all fit as they are.
