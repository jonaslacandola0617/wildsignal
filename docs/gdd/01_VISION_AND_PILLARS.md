# Chapter 1 — Vision and pillars

**Status:** DRAFT, written from the owner's answers of 2026-11-02. Awaiting owner approval to LOCK.

## 1.1 The pitch

**Wildsignal is a cozy, open-world creature adventure: roam a beautiful archipelago, battle wild Signari in fast real-time fights, and tune in to their signals to bring them onto your team — while uncovering why the islands' ancient signal has gone quiet.**

Shorter, for a store page or a friend: *"The warmth of the old Pokémon games, with real-time fights and a world you can wander freely."*

## 1.2 The feeling we're after

Players should come away **happy**: the joy of a new creature joining the team, of a hard fight won, of finding a hidden corner of the map. The owner's reference point is the feeling of the classic Pokémon games — **remembered, never copied**.

Three moods take turns, and none of them carries the game alone:

| Mood | Where players feel it |
|---|---|
| **Cozy** | Villages, sunny meadows, companions following you, resting at a cottage |
| **Action** | Real-time fights: dodging, timing commands, swapping, wearing a wild Signari down |
| **Mystery** | Ruins, strange signals, Signari nobody has recorded, what lies under the islands |

The rule of thumb: a cozy stretch is never long without something to fight or discover, and a tense moment always ends somewhere safe and warm.

## 1.3 Who it's for

Everyone, with three groups in mind:

- **Teens and adults who grew up on Pokémon** and want that feeling again with something new.
- **Fans of Palworld and Cassette Beasts** who want creatures plus more active, physical play.
- **Indie game players** who like handmade worlds and charming pixel art.

It must stay friendly for younger players too: no gore, no frightening cruelty, readable text.

## 1.4 Pillars

Every feature is checked against these four. If it doesn't serve at least one, it doesn't go in.

1. **Fights that feel good.** Real-time, readable, skill-based. You move, roll and dodge while commanding your Signari. Matchups and team choices matter.
2. **A world worth wandering.** Connected regions you roam freely. Every path leads somewhere: a new Signari, a secret, a view, a piece of the story.
3. **Creatures that are truly original.** Every Signari comes from the owner's own hand-drawn designs. Each has a personality and behaviour you can see in the world, not just numbers.
4. **Kind at heart.** Signari faint, they never die. You recruit them by earning them in a fight and tuning in to their signal, not by trapping them. Cozy places stay cozy.

## 1.5 What makes Wildsignal different

- **Real-time fights where you are on the field too:** you dodge attacks aimed at you while directing your Signari.
- **Recruiting is a skill moment, not a dice roll:** wear the Signari down without knocking it out, then hold steady while you tune in.
- **The Resonance Compass** (proposed; detailed in chapter 2): the Wayfinder's tool for finding Signari and tuning in to their signals.
- **A hand-drawn roster of original creatures**, shown in a stylised pixel-3D world.

## 1.6 Things Wildsignal never does

- No death or gore. Signari faint and recover.
- No pay-to-win. Purchases (if any) are cosmetic only and never change stats, recruiting or progress.
- No gacha, loot boxes or paid random rewards.
- No copying Pokémon or any other game's names, creatures, art, interface or items (no capture balls).
- No forced grinding. The story never requires repeating fights just to level up.
- No energy timers or "come back tomorrow" waits.
- The full story is always playable offline.
- No ads interrupting play.
- No breeding or selling Signari.
- Never replacing or AI-redrawing the owner's original creature designs.

## 1.7 Release scope (version 1.0)

| Item | Target | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Regions | 6, connected | As planned since 2026-10-20 |
| Signari | **50** | Raised from 18–24 by the owner on 2026-11-02 for a wider roster. See the risk below. |
| Main story | 3–5 hours | Collecting all 50 and optional areas add more |
| Platforms | Windows (64-bit) and Android | Minimum devices: i3 10th-gen / 4 GB PC, Galaxy S10+ |
| Online | **After launch** | 1.0 is single-player; two-player co-op moves to a later update |
| Business | Free-to-play, cosmetic-only purchases | As decided on 2026-10-20; not needed for 1.0 if purchases ship later |

**Scope risk, stated plainly:** 50 Signari is the biggest cost in the game. Each one needs a game-ready 3D model with animations, a move kit, a habitat and balancing. Today only a handful have game models. Chapter 4 will plan how creatures are produced; if that plan shows 50 can't be made well in time, the owner decides between a later release or fewer Signari at launch. Quality comes before quantity.
