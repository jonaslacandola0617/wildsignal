# Region 1 story — Act 1 opening

**Status:** text OK'd by the owner on 2026-11-09 (D-094). The game reads it from `data/content/story.json`.

Built from GDD chapter 3 (the locked beats). Short lines; each fits the two-line dialogue box. The Wayfinder never speaks aloud: the choices only change the tone of the answer, never the plot.

Words in *italics* are plain narration.

## 1. Arrival

*The boat from the mainland takes three days.*
*On the third morning the fog lifts over Lantern Quay.*
*People here say the islands used to hum.*

**Mara:** There you are! In one piece, too. The last Wayfinder was sick on the jetty.
**Mara:** Mara Venn. I'll be showing you around Hearthgrove.

> - "Glad to be here." → **Mara:** Good. Hold on to that.
> - "It's so quiet." → **Mara:** Quieter than it was. Thirty years quieter.
> - *(Nod.)* → **Mara:** A person of few words. The Signari will like you.

**Mara:** You'll hear about the Hush. Every station went silent in one night.
**Mara:** Signari scattered. Villages lost each other. Ask ten people why, you'll get ten answers.
**Mara:** Now the signal's coming back, in pieces. That's why we need you.
**Mara:** Go and see Old Fen at the Workshop. Touch nothing.

## 2. The compass

**Old Fen:** Ah! New hands. Don't touch that. Or that.
**Old Fen:** That one you can touch. It's already broken.
**Old Fen:** Here. Resonance Compass. I built it myself, so it's probably fine.

*You received the Resonance Compass.*

**Old Fen:** It won't tell you where to go. It will tell you when something is listening.

> - "How does it work?" → **Old Fen:** Badly, at first. Then it learns you.
> - "Thank you." → **Old Fen:** Hmph. Say that when it works.

**Old Fen:** Nobody walks these meadows alone. Mara! Bring the little ones!

## 3. Your first Signari

**Mara:** These two have been waiting for someone.
**Mara:** Choose the one you'd want beside you on a bad day.

> - **Glintle** → **Mara:** Glintle. Be patient with it. It's braver than it looks.
> - **Driftfin** → **Mara:** Driftfin. It will get you wet. You'll forgive it.
> - **Brumlet** *(needs a model first)* → **Mara:** Brumlet. Keep it away from the thatch.

## 4. The first signal you answer

**Mara:** The meadows are waking up. Find a wild Signari and go gently.
**Mara:** Wear it down, get close, and hold the compass steady. Hold F.
**Mara:** We never take a Signari. We listen until it answers.

*Goal: tune in a wild Signari.*

**Mara:** It answered you. Do you know how long that took me, my first time?

> - "How long?" → **Mara:** Longer than I'd tell Fen.
> - "It was scared." → **Mara:** Yes. Remember that. Most people forget.

**Mara:** That field past the well is yours, by the way. Plant something.
**Mara:** A Wayfinder needs somewhere to come back to.

## 5. A favour for Old Fen

**Old Fen:** Compass parts rattle. I need moss to pack them. Soft Moss, two handfuls.

*Goal: bring Old Fen 2 Soft Moss.*

**Old Fen:** Lovely. Damp. Slightly alive. Here, for your trouble.

*You received 40 coins.*

## 6. Juno

**Juno:** You're the new one! Juno Hale. Also new. But better.
**Juno:** First one to the top of the Wayfinder list wins. Starting now.

> - "You're on." → **Juno:** Ha! Good.
> - "There's a list?" → **Juno:** There's about to be.

*Fight: Juno and Hornkid. (Juno's Signari can't be tuned in.)*

If you win:
**Juno:** Huh. Okay. Lucky.
**Juno:** ...That wasn't luck, was it. Next time, then.

If you lose:
**Juno:** Ha! Go and rest. I'll be right here, being better.

## 7. Something is listening

*The compass shivers in your hand.*
*Far off in the grove, something is listening. It sounds afraid.*

**Mara:** The Singing Root. There's a guardian there, old as the village.
**Mara:** Go carefully. And come back and tell me what you see.

*Goal: go to the Singing Root. Fight: Logwarden, the bound grove guardian.*

*A small iron bell hangs from its bark. It will not stop ringing.*

After you win:

*The bell cracks and falls. Slowly, the shaking stops.*
*For the first time in a long while, its signal comes through. It stays to guard the grove.*

## 8. The way on

**Mara:** A bell? Show me.
**Mara:** ...Nobody in Hearthgrove makes bells like this.

> - "You've seen one before." → **Mara:** Get some rest. We'll talk about Liltwood.
> - "Who would do this?" → **Mara:** Someone who thinks they're helping. That's the worst kind.

**Old Fen:** Give me that compass. Hm. It's found a second voice. Coming from Liltwood.
**Old Fen:** There. Now it'll hear further. Mind you, so will they.

*End of the region 1 opening. Liltwood comes with region 2.*
